Fill in `[Name]`, `[time]` and `[place]` before sending. Send it **this evening**
— late afternoon or early evening is when people actually check and reply.

## Why it's written this way

**Lead with the confirmation, not the pitch.** The first line answers the only
question they opened the message to answer.

**"I built it" is the whole hook.** It reframes the meeting from *listening to a
salesperson* to *reviewing something that already exists* — much lower
resistance, and it's the reason to turn up.

**Name the length.** "About 30 minutes" removes the fear of losing an afternoon.

**Ask for exactly one thing.** Their real prices. It's a small ask, it makes the
meeting concrete, and someone who brings prices has already half-decided.

**One emoji per paragraph at most.** Warm, not junior.

**Don't send the price.** J$100,000 lands in the room, after they've seen the
work — not in a message they read at a traffic light.
