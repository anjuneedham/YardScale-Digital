Privacy

Privacy
policy

Short, because we collect very little. This covers this website only — AgriLoop has its own privacy policy inside the app.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Who we are

YardScale Digital, an app studio based in Jamaica, founded by Andrew Needham. For anything in this policy, write to yardscaledigital@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Newsletter signups. Your email address, and your first name if you choose to give it. That's all the form asks for, and we don't ask for more later.
  • Email you send us. Whatever is in the message you send to the address above.
  • Standard server logs. Our host records ordinary request data, including IP addresses, to serve the site and block abuse.

There are no advertising trackers, no analytics scripts and no cookies set by this site.

Why we collect it

Your email address is used to send you the build log — occasional updates about the studio, its products and what we learn building them. We don't send anything else to that list, and we don't sell, rent or share it.

Who processes it

  • Resend stores the mailing list and sends the emails on our behalf.
  • Netlify hosts this website and runs the signup endpoint.

Both act as service providers processing data for us under their own terms. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your email stays on the list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it, at which point it is deleted from the audience.

Your choices

  • Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, and it works immediately.
  • You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing yardscaledigital@gmail.com. We'll action it within 30 days.

Children

This site isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and say so in the build log.
