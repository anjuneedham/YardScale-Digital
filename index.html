The studio

An app studio
built around growth

We don't want to build software and hope somebody finds it. Our background is growth, so a product gets judged on distribution and retention from the first week — not after launch, when it's expensive to be wrong.

That's the whole advantage of the studio: the people deciding what to build are the people who already know what it takes to get it used. Build and grow, by the same small team.

  • Need A problem someone already works around by hand, not one we invented for a pitch.
  • Product Shipped small and honest — if something isn't built yet, the app says so.
  • Reach How the first hundred users actually hear about it, decided before we build.
  • Retention Whether they come back next week, which is the only number that settles it.
  • Iterate Narrow releases, real usage, and the willingness to cut what nobody touches.

Our first product

AgriLoop

Invite-only beta · Jamaica

Connecting Caribbean farmers with the people who want what they grow.

A Jamaica-first agricultural marketplace. Farmers list what they have — a photo, a price, a unit, a parish — and buyers search the parishes closest to them. Payment stays between the two of them; AgriLoop is where the deal gets found and agreed.

  • Listings with search, filters and parish-level browsing
  • Farmer, business and buyer profiles with reviews and verification
  • Buyer requests, quotes, orders and invoices
  • Direct messaging between farmers and buyers
  • A community board and a learning academy
  • Web and Android, built mobile-first

The journey

How a growth agency
became a studio

Five stages, in the order they actually happened. We're at the fifth.

01

Growth

YardScale began by helping businesses reach customers and build growth systems — content, sites, automation, the work that gets a small operation found.

02

Learning

Working that close to businesses exposed the same problems again and again: the recurring friction, the markets nobody was serving, the workarounds people had quietly accepted.

03

Building

We started turning those observations into software instead of campaigns — the point where the work changed from marketing somebody else's product to building one.

04

App studio

YardScale is becoming a studio focused on building, launching and growing its own products. That's the transition this site is describing, and it is still underway.

05

AgriLoop

Our first product. An agricultural marketplace starting in Jamaica, in invite-only beta now, designed from the start with the rest of the Caribbean in mind.

Founder

Andrew
Needham

YardScale Digital Founder

Andrew started YardScale in growth and marketing — helping businesses reach customers and build the systems behind that. The pattern that kept showing up in that work was simple: the problem usually wasn't the marketing. It was that the right product didn't exist yet.

So the work changed. YardScale now builds products of its own, from Jamaica, with the Caribbean market in mind — a region big enough to matter and small enough to be ignored by everyone building elsewhere. AgriLoop is the first of those products, and the studio is being built one shipped thing at a time.
