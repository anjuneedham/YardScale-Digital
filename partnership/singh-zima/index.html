03 · Your businesses and growth opportunities

Where I'd start looking.

Starting points for our conversation, based on what's public and what you've told me. Tap any one to open it.

Linkio

PublicSold locally · from our chat

NFC business cards. Tap the card on a phone and the holder's contact details and profile open. Profile pages are free to build, and your descriptions invite viewers to "build your first site for free."

  • PositioningPick the first buyer+

    Individuals, sales teams, small businesses, tradespeople. Choosing one to lead with makes the site, the content and the price easier to get right. The others can follow.

  • FunnelFree page → card order+

    The free page is a strong way in. The useful number is how many free pages turn into card orders, and where people drop off in between. That tells us what to fix first.

  • OnboardingGet every card set up and tapped+

    A card only works for the buyer, and only spreads Linkio, once it's set up and used. A short setup flow and a "first tap" reminder are cheap to test.

  • Referral loopEvery tap is a referral moment+

    The person on the receiving end of a tap sees Linkio. If the profile page gives them a clear way to get their own, each card becomes a small acquisition channel. I'd want to check what the page shows today before suggesting changes.

  • MonetizationTeam and business orders+

    One business buying cards for ten staff is worth more than ten single sales, and easier to win. A simple team offer would be a quick test.

  • Content salesShow real customers using it+

    Short clips of real customers tapping their cards at work, filmed the way you already film. Each one doubles as proof and as a demo.

Your finance app

From our chat · in development

A finance app you're building for a global audience. I haven't seen it yet and haven't found public details, so these are questions and areas to explore, not prescriptions.

  • PositioningChoose a first market+

    Global is the destination. Which country and which kind of user comes first? A narrow start makes it easier to learn quickly and to get the message right.

  • AcquisitionA waitlist or beta from your channel+

    Your audience could fill an early beta. A waitlist with one or two short questions shows who's actually interested and why, before money goes into ads.

  • OnboardingEarn trust early+

    People are careful with money apps. Plain explanations of security and data use, plus a quick path to the first useful moment, tend to decide whether someone stays.

  • MonetizationDecide pricing with real usage+

    Subscription, fees, or free with a paid tier. Those are easier to choose once a beta shows how people actually use the app.

  • RetentionHabit before spend+

    Finance apps live on regular use. Measure how many people come back each week before spending on acquisition, then add referrals once they do.
