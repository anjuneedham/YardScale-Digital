01 · The marks

One symbol, four lockups

A balance beam tipped twelve degrees, resting on a fulcrum: a scale that is rising rather than level. The gold pivot is the point of sale — the single moment everything in this business turns on. Reach for the horizontal lockup by default; the others exist for the places it will not fit.

When to use which

Horizontal for anything wider than it is tall — websites, letterheads, vehicle doors, email signatures. Stacked where the space is square or narrow. Symbol alone only where the name is already present or obvious: app icons, social avatars, a stamp on a receipt.

Give it room,
keep it legible

Define X as the cap height of the wordmark — the height of the letter S. Keep at least X clear on every side, free of type, images, rules and the edge of the page.

04 · Type

Three faces, all free

All three are open-source and load from Google Fonts, so the brand costs nothing to run and anyone can pick it up. The wordmark itself is drawn from Sora ExtraBold and shipped as outlines — the logo files need no font installed.

Display · Sora · 700 & 800
Every sale, counted.

Headlines, the wordmark, buttons. Tight tracking at large sizes (−3 to −4.5%). Never below 600 weight — Sora goes limp when it is light.

Body · Inter · 400, 500 & 600
Ring it up, bank it, know what you have left on the shelf — the till does the counting.

Everything you read in a paragraph. Line height 1.55–1.65. Set body copy at 16–17px on screen; do not go under 15px.

Labels & figures · JetBrains Mono · 500
POINT OF SALE · SETUP · SUPPORT
J$11,500   15%   02:14

Eyebrows, small caps labels, and every price or figure. Letterspace uppercase labels to about 0.18em. It is the receipt voice — use it where the system is speaking.

If the fonts are unavailable

Fall back to the system stack in this order: Sora → system-ui → Segoe UI → Helvetica. Do not substitute a different display face on a whim; a wrong headline font undoes the mark faster than a wrong colour.

05 · Getting it wrong

Six things not to do

Every one of these has happened to a logo somewhere. Each takes ten seconds to do and quietly costs the brand its consistency.

Don't stretch itScale proportionally, always. Hold shift.
Don't recolour itGreen, coconut, solid black or solid white. Nothing else.
Don't tilt itThe beam is already at an angle. That is the whole idea.
Don't drop it on a busy or close-toned groundUse the reversed file on dark.
Don't crowd itKeep the clear space on page 3, even in a tight header.
Do just use the fileEvery lockup you need is already drawn. Nothing needs rebuilding.

Also, please

Do not add a drop shadow, an outline, a gradient, or a second colour to the wordmark. Do not typeset the name yourself in place of the wordmark. Do not put the symbol inside another shape — the tile file already does that properly.

Need something not in here?

A one-colour foil, an embroidery file, a vehicle wrap at scale, a large-format print — ask rather than redraw. All of it comes off the same vector in minutes.
