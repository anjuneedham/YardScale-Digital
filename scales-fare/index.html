Straight answers

The questions we get asked

What happens when the light cut?

You keep selling. The terminal holds charge and the software stores the sale on the machine, so the queue never stops. When power and internet return, everything uploads by itself — you don't press anything. That's why we put a UPS on the counter as standard rather than as an upsell.

Do I need internet for it to work?

Not to make a sale. You need a connection for the nightly sync, for reports on your phone, and for card authorisation through your bank terminal. A basic fixed line is ideal; a mobile hotspot is a perfectly good backup and we set that up as part of install day.

Does it handle GCT properly?

Yes. You set prices the way you already quote them — GCT-inclusive or exclusive — and the receipt shows the breakdown along with your business name, address and TRN. Reports separate taxable from zero-rated items so filing isn't a reconstruction exercise. We'd still say confirm your filing setup with your accountant or Tax Administration Jamaica; we build the system, we don't file for you.

Can I still take card if I already have a bank terminal?

Yes, and you should. We work alongside the terminal you already have from NCB, Scotiabank, JN, Sagicor or whoever you bank with, so you keep the merchant rate you negotiated. The till records the payment type; the money still settles through your bank exactly as it does today.

What about the customers who buy on trust?

The credit book comes into the system. Each customer gets an account, what they owe is on the screen when they walk in, and you can print or WhatsApp a statement instead of arguing over a notebook. Set a limit per customer and the till warns the cashier before it goes past it.

Do you actually come to the shop?

Yes — install day happens in person anywhere on the island, and we build the setup before we travel so the visit is short. If you're elsewhere in the Caribbean we ship the kit configured and do the training over a call, then stay on hand for the first month of trading.

My staff aren't computer people. Is that a problem?

No. The selling screen is buttons and pictures — most cashiers are confident inside an hour. The complicated parts are set up by us and locked behind a manager login, so nobody can change a price or void a sale without it showing up in the day's report.
