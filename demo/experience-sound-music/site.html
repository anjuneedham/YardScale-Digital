What we bring

Four things,
one booking

Hire the piece you need or the whole room. Either way it is one contact, one invoice and one crew who have worked together before.

01

Live band

A working band that reads the room and keeps the floor moving, from the first dance to the last set.

  • Trios up to full horn sections
  • Reggae, soca, gospel, soul, pop
  • Your song list learned in advance
  • MC and vocals included
02

PA & sound systems

Rigs sized to the room and the guest count, delivered, set up and run by someone who stays for the night.

  • Speeches, ceremonies and full stage
  • Indoor, outdoor and marquee
  • Wireless mics and monitors
  • Delivery, setup and breakdown
03

DJs

Selectors who can hold a wedding and a warehouse, and know which one they are at.

  • Full night or between band sets
  • Requests and do‑not‑play lists honoured
  • Own decks and backline
  • Dancefloor lighting available
04

Event crew

The people who make the rest invisible — load in, run the desk, handle the changeovers, load out.

  • Sound engineer on site all night
  • Stage and cable management
  • Run sheet agreed beforehand
  • Backup gear on the truck
