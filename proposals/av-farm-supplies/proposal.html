The short version

Your landing page is built. Over the next thirty days we put it to work: your existing photos and video reformatted and posted on a schedule that holds, paid campaigns behind the pieces that perform, and every lead that comes back captured in one place with a name, a number and what they asked about. One fixed price of JMD $40,000 — half to start, half on launch. At day 30 you get the numbers and a written next step, and you can carry on month to month.

What's included

Web

The landing page, live and yours

  • One page with one job — turn a visit into an enquiry
  • Mobile-first and built to load fast on local mobile data
  • Enquiry form plus click-to-chat straight into your WhatsApp
  • Your products, prices and delivery areas laid out plainly
  • Hosting configured, domain connected, analytics wired in
  • Handed over documented — nothing you run depends on us
Organic

Your content, posted properly

  • Built from the content you already have — product shots, yard photos, video
  • Reformatted, captioned and cut for each platform
  • A posting calendar built and run by us for the full 30 days
  • Hooks and angles tested against each other, not guessed at
  • What performs gets made again; what doesn't gets dropped
Paid

Advertising behind what works

  • Meta campaigns — Facebook and Instagram — built, launched and managed
  • Audiences targeted by parish, farming type and buying intent
  • Ad creative cut from your existing content, not stock
  • Monitored daily, with budget moved to the ads that convert
  • Retargeting for people who hit the page and didn't enquire

Ad spend is paid by AV Farm Supplies directly to Meta and sits outside this quote. You set the daily budget and you keep control of it.

Leads

Lead generation and handover

  • Every enquiry captured in one place — a shared sheet or your inbox, your call
  • Name, contact, source and what they were asking about
  • Reply templates so nothing sits unanswered overnight
  • Weekly check-in on what's coming in and what it's costing
  • Day-30 report: leads, cost per lead, best-performing content, next step
