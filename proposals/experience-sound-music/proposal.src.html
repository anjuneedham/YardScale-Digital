Where this came from

You are already
doing the hard part

You reached out to us directly. The message was clear about what you offer — a live band, PA systems, DJs and a support team that knows how to run a room — and it ended with a link. That is outreach most businesses never get around to doing, and you are doing it by hand, one conversation at a time.

Then the link goes to an about page.

Someone with an event in six weeks reads your message, gets interested enough to tap, and arrives somewhere that tells them who you are rather than asking them what they need. There is nothing wrong with that page. It is just not the page that converts the click you worked for. The outreach is working; the destination is not built to catch it.

01

The effort is manual

Every enquiry costs you a message you typed yourself. That is real work, and right now it is the only thing bringing people in.

02

The click has nowhere to go

An about page answers “who are you”. It does not answer “are you free on the 14th, and what will it cost me”.

03

Nothing is captured

People who tap and leave are gone. No name, no date, no event type, nothing to follow up on next week.

What we propose

One page,
one job

We build you a single booking page and point every link you send at it — the DMs, the bio, the posts, the flyers. It opens fast on a phone, because a phone is what your traffic is on. It says what you do, shows the four things you hire out, and then does the one thing your current destination does not: it asks for the date.

Two ways to reach you from that page. A short enquiry form for the people who want to think, and a WhatsApp button for the people who want to talk now. Both land with you the same day, with the details already attached.

This is a one‑off build, fixed price, fully handed over. When it is done it is yours — the files, the hosting account, the domain, all of it. Nothing you run afterwards depends on us.

Scope of work

What gets built

S‑01

The booking page

A single page, built mobile‑first: the offer up top, your four services broken out — live band, PA and sound systems, DJs, event crew — and a clear path to enquire from anywhere on the page.

S‑02

Enquiry form that asks the right questions

Event date, event type, venue or parish, rough guest count, and which services they need. It arrives in your inbox already qualified, so your first reply can be a real answer instead of five questions.

S‑03

One‑tap WhatsApp booking

A button that opens WhatsApp with the message part‑written. The person only has to add their date and hit send — which is the difference between an enquiry and a page view.

S‑04

Gallery and proof

A section for your photos, video and past events, plus room for client quotes. Built to take the material you already have — we will tell you exactly what to send and what is missing.

S‑05

Link previews that actually render

Right now your link travels through WhatsApp and Instagram as bare text. We set the title, description and preview image so the URL unfurls into a proper card every time you paste it.

S‑06

Found on search, locally

Page titles, descriptions and structure set up for the terms people actually type — sound system rental, live band hire, DJ for hire — plus your Google Business Profile claimed and pointed at the new page.

S‑07

Hosting, domain and speed

Deployed on managed hosting under your own account, wired to your domain with SSL. Built as a static page so it loads in about a second on mobile data, and costs nothing per month to keep online.

S‑08

Handover and a walkthrough

A live session where we hand you the accounts and show you how it works, plus written notes for changing your own copy, prices and photos later without calling anyone.
