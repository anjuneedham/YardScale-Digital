Where this came from

Right now, you are
the whole system

You reached out to us directly. The message was clear about what you offer — a live band, PA systems, DJs and a support team that knows how to run a room — and it ended with a link. That is outreach most businesses never get around to doing.

It is also the problem. Every enquiry you get costs you a message you typed yourself, so the number of leads your business can produce is capped by the number of messages one person can send in a day. The link goes to an about page, so the people who do tap arrive somewhere that tells them who you are instead of asking them what they need. And when someone decides at eleven at night that they need a band for the 14th, nobody answers until morning.

Nothing here is broken. It is just all being done by hand, by you. This proposal replaces that with four things that run whether you are working or not, and routes every one of them into the same place.

01

The reach is capped

Outreach only happens on the days you have time to do it. Nothing is working on the days you are running an event.

02

The click has nowhere to go

An about page answers “who are you”. It does not answer “are you free on the 14th, and what will it cost me”.

03

Nothing is captured

People who tap and leave are gone. No name, no date, no event type, nothing to follow up on next week.

What we propose

One system,
four inputs

Organic content and paid ads put you in front of people who are planning events. Both send them to a website built to take a booking rather than tell a story. That site, and every DM that arrives on WhatsApp, Instagram or TikTok, feeds into an agent trained on your prices, your packages and your tone — which answers immediately, at any hour, in your voice.

The agent handles the questions you answer fifty times a month. It takes the date, the venue, the guest count and the services they need. Then it passes you the people worth your time, with the details already attached — and answers everyone else without involving you at all.

Scope of work

What gets built

PART 01 The website
W‑01

The booking page

Built mobile‑first, because a phone is what your traffic is on. The offer up top, your four services broken out — live band, PA and sound systems, DJs, event crew — and a clear way to enquire from anywhere on the page.

W‑02

An enquiry form that qualifies

Event date, event type, venue or parish, rough guest count, services needed. It arrives already answered, so your first reply can be a price instead of five questions.

W‑03

Gallery and proof

A section for your photos, video and past events, with room for client quotes. We will tell you exactly what to send and what is missing.

W‑04

Hosting, domain, speed and link previews

Deployed under your own account, wired to your domain with SSL, and built static so it opens in about a second on mobile data. Preview cards set so the URL unfurls properly every time it is pasted into WhatsApp or Instagram.

PART 02 The agent
A‑01

A DM agent on WhatsApp, Instagram and TikTok

It answers every message that arrives, in seconds, at any hour. The enquiry that lands at eleven at night gets a real reply at eleven at night rather than whenever you next open your phone.

A‑02

Trained on your business, in your voice

Your prices, packages, service areas, availability rules and the questions you get asked most, written the way you would write them. We build this from a working session with you and refine it against real conversations before it goes live.

A‑03

Qualifies, quotes and books

It takes the date, venue, guest count and services, gives your standard pricing where the rules are clear, and hands you the people worth your time with the details attached. Anything outside its rules is escalated to you rather than guessed at.

PART 03 Organic
O‑01

A content system and a 30‑day calendar

What to post, when, and why — built around what you actually do in a week. Formats and hooks chosen for your market, not a generic template, and simple enough that you can keep running it after handover.

O‑02

A first batch of content, produced

We cut, caption and title the opening month from footage you already have, so the system launches with content in it rather than an empty calendar and good intentions.

O‑03

Profiles set up to convert

Bio, link, highlights and pinned posts across your pages, all pointed at the new site. Google Business Profile claimed and completed so you turn up when someone searches for sound system or band hire near them.

PART 04 Paid
P‑01

Campaigns built and launched

Meta ads across Instagram and Facebook, targeted at people planning events in the parishes you serve. Audiences, budgets and structure set up in an ad account registered in your name.

P‑02

Ad creative, and more than one of it

Several versions built from your material and run against each other, so within the first weeks you know which offer and which cut people respond to instead of guessing.

P‑03

Tracking, so you can see what worked

Conversion tracking wired from the ad through to the enquiry. You will be able to see which campaign produced which booking — which is the only thing that makes an ad budget a decision rather than a gamble.

PART 05 Handover
H‑01

One place everything lands

Website enquiries, agent conversations and ad leads routed into a single inbox with the same details on each, so working your leads is one list and not four apps.

H‑02

Accounts in your name, and a walkthrough

A live session where we hand over every account — hosting, domain, ad account, agent — and show you how the parts fit, plus written notes for changing your own copy, prices and agent answers later without calling anyone.
